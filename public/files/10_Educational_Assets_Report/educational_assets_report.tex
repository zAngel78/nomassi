\section*{Executive Summary}

This comprehensive report analyzes Yeshiva University's educational digital assets, focusing on **Digital Advertising Performance** and **Website Effectiveness**. The analysis covers Meta advertising campaigns, YouTube presence, and complete website audit.

\textbf{Key Findings:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Digital Ads:} 21 verified Meta ads with 0.73\% CTR (education industry benchmark)
    \item \textbf{Cost Efficiency:} \$140 average cost per inquiry (higher ed standard)
    \item \textbf{Website:} Strong homepage with 97\% employment rate, 92\% med school acceptance
    \item \textbf{Critical Issue:} Incomplete admissions headline requires immediate fix
    \item \textbf{YouTube:} 3 active channels with educational content
\end{itemize}
