## 4. Strategic Recommendations

### 4.1 Digital Advertising Optimization

**High Priority:**
1. **Increase Video Ad Investment** (Current: 57% → Target: 75%)
   - Video ads perform 2.3x better than static (2.1% vs 0.9% CTR)
   - Recommended budget shift: +$15K/month to video production
   - Expected ROI: 180% within 6 months

2. **Expand TikTok Advertising** (Current: $0 → Target: $8K/month)
   - TikTok engagement rate: 4.80% (3.2x higher than Instagram)
   - Gen Z reach: 60% of prospective students
   - Estimated cost per inquiry: $85 (40% lower than current $140)

3. **A/B Testing Program**
   - Test student testimonials vs faculty interviews
   - Test NYC imagery vs campus shots
   - Test different CTAs ("Apply Now" vs "Learn More" vs "Schedule Visit")

**Medium Priority:**
4. **Retargeting Campaigns**
   - Target website visitors who didn't convert
   - Estimated conversion lift: 25-35%
   - Budget: $5K/month

5. **LinkedIn Ads for Graduate Programs**
   - Currently underutilized (only 6 ads)
   - Professional audience targeting
   - Budget: $10K/month for Azrieli/Katz programs

### 4.2 YouTube Strategy Enhancement

**Immediate Actions:**
1. **Increase Upload Frequency** (Current: 2-3/week → Target: 4-5/week)
   - Focus on short-form content (< 5 minutes)
   - Student vlogs, day-in-the-life series
   - Faculty research highlights

2. **Optimize Video SEO**
   - Better titles, descriptions, tags
   - Custom thumbnails (current: auto-generated)
   - Playlist organization by topic

3. **Cross-Promote Secondary Channels**
   - Leverage Maccabeats' 36K subscribers
   - Collaborative content between channels
   - Unified branding across all channels

**Expected Results:**
- Subscriber growth: 5,810 → 12,000 (12 months)
- Average views/video: 1,200 → 3,500
- Engagement rate: 2.3% → 4.5%

### 4.3 Website Improvements

**Minor Enhancements:**
1. **Homepage Load Speed** (Current: 2.1s → Target: < 1.5s)
   - Image optimization (WebP format)
   - Lazy loading for below-fold content
   - CDN implementation

2. **Donation Page Integration**
   - Reduce friction from 301 redirect
   - Embed GiveCampus iframe directly on yu.edu
   - Maintain brand consistency throughout donation flow

3. **Alumni Page Expansion**
   - Add more success stories (current: limited)
   - Interactive alumni map (70K+ worldwide)
   - Career networking features

**Analytics Enhancement:**
- Implement heatmapping (Hotjar/Crazy Egg)
- Set up conversion funnels in Google Analytics
- Track micro-conversions (video plays, downloads, etc.)

### 4.4 Press & Media Expansion

**Recommendations:**
1. **Diversify Media Partnerships**
   - Current: WSJ only ($210K-$354K/year)
   - Add: New York Times, Chronicle of Higher Education
   - Budget: +$150K/year for additional placements

2. **Increase Press Release Frequency**
   - Current: 2-3/month
   - Target: 4-5/month (weekly cadence)
   - Focus on research breakthroughs, student achievements

3. **Media Training for Faculty**
   - Position faculty as thought leaders
   - Increase media mentions and citations
   - Build relationships with education reporters
