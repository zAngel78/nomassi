How to Collect Ad Data (Step by Step)

  1. Click on each link below - Opens Meta Ad Library or Google Ads
  2. Verify you're human - Complete any CAPTCHA if prompted
  3. Browse active ads - See all current advertising campaigns
  4. Copy ad details:
    • Ad text/copy
    • Images/videos (screenshot or save URL)
    • Call-to-action buttons
    • Started running date
    • Platforms (Facebook/Instagram/etc)
  5. Save to template - Use meta_ads_data_template.json

Important Tips

  • Legal & Public: All data from Meta Ad Library and Google Ads Transparency is 100% public and legal to collect
  • No Automation: Both platforms require human verification (CAPTCHA) to prevent scraping
  • Save Screenshots: Take screenshots of ads for visual reference in your research
  • Note Targeting: Pay attention to age ranges, locations, and interests used in targeting
  • Track Timing: Notice when ads started running and how long they've been active
  • Compare Strategies: Look for differences in messaging, visuals, and calls-to-action between universities
  • Use Template: Fill in meta_ads_data_template.json with your findings for structured data
